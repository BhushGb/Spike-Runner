SPIKE RUNNER PLAYER GUIDE
CHANGE LOG: from Casual Guide to MS Style Compliant Guide 

1. CLARITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Moved the Game Over consequence earlier in section 5.05: instead of
  ending the icon row with a passive aside ("The collided spike is
  destroyed."), the compliant version immediately tells the reader the
  outcome that matters most: "If square's strength is 0, then one red
  spike collision will result in Game Over." This puts the most
  important information first, right after the visual that illustrates
  it.
- Changed the vague heading "What Happens When You Collect It?" to
  "When You Collect Yellow Gravity Feed:" so the subject is explicit
  instead of relying on the reader to infer what "It" refers to.
- Changed "Strength 0 (X appears)" to "Strength = 0" (a precise,
  unambiguous mathematical statement instead of a parenthetical aside).

2. CONCISENESS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Combined the three separate sentences under "1. Introduction" into
  one paragraph.
- Removed the redundant Getting Started step "You can also drag and
  drop the Spike Runner.html file directly into an open browser
  window."
- Removed the redundant sentence "You can use mouse click or Enter to
  proceed further" from section 3.02 (repeats information already
  given).
- Shortened the Green Strength Fuel description from three sentences
  ("...black '+' symbol. It is intentionally larger than a tiny pickup
  but remains small enough to be a jumpable gameplay object. It is not
  a spike.") to one: "The green object is a medium-sized green circle
  with a black plus symbol."
- Changed "Every red spike collision removes exactly 1 Strength:" to
  "Every red spike collision removes 1 strength." (dropped the
  unnecessary qualifier "exactly").
- Removed the duplicate "Damaged player (0 Strength)" image, which
  repeated a visual already shown in the Strength Collision icon row.
- Removed one of two redundant instances of "OFFICIAL PLAYER GUIDE" on
  the cover page (the casual guide repeats it once under the title and
  again near the bottom).
- Shortened heading "5.07 Scoring and High Score" to "5.07 High Score."

3. ACCURACY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- No factual errors found in either version; both describe the same
  game rules (Strength starts and caps at 3, gravity states, control
  keys). No changes were needed under this category beyond the
  terminology fixes captured under Consistency.
- Confirmed the Controls table and the Quick Reference table describe
  the same key bindings in both versions—no discrepancies introduced
  by editing.

4. CONSISTENCY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Changed inconsistent internal capitalization of the official term
  "Strength Fuel"—for example "Collect green Strength fuel" and
  "Green Strength fuel" in the casual guide—to a single consistent
  form, "Green Strength Fuel," used the same way in headings, bullets,
  and captions.
- Changed "Strength" to lowercase "strength" everywhere it's used as a
  common noun (not the two-word term "Strength Fuel" and not at the
  start of a sentence), for example "Maintain your Strength" to
  "Maintain your strength," and "-1 Strength" / "+1 Strength" to
  "-1 strength" / "+1 strength" in the Quick Reference table.
- Changed Title Case table values that are generic descriptions, not
  proper names—"Red Spike," "Red Spike Collision," "Yellow Square,"
  "Green Circle," "Fatal Collision," "Red Hazard"—to sentence case:
  "Red spike," "Red spike collision," "Yellow square," "Green circle,"
  "Fatal collision," "Red hazard." At the same time, true proper names
  in the same table—"Gravity Feed" and "Strength Fuel"—were correctly
  left capitalized, showing a deliberate, consistent line between
  generic terms and official object names.
- Standardized "Theme Selection" capitalization (the casual guide
  mixes "Theme Selection screen" and "Theme selection" for the same
  screen).
- Standardized heading capitalization scheme across the whole document
  (see Capitalization, below) so every heading and subheading follows
  the same pattern.

5. ACCESSIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Removed the red-colored, bold styling applied to the words "Red
  spikes" at the start of section 5.02, since color was the only cue
  distinguishing that text—readers who can't perceive color would miss
  the emphasis entirely. The text is now plain, matching the rest of
  the paragraph.
- Removed bold formatting from "blue square" in the player description,
  which added emphasis without adding meaning.
- Removed the decorative full-width red banner graphic that appeared
  across the top of section-opening pages in the casual guide (for
  example, above "5. Gameplay"). It carried no information and added
  visual clutter; the compliant version presents the same heading
  directly on a plain background.
- Rewrote image captions as complete descriptive phrases instead of
  Title Case fragments—for example, "Bottom Gravity — White Theme
  (player mid-jump over red spikes)" became "Bottom gravity, white
  theme: the player mid-jump over red spikes"—so a screen reader (or a
  reader who can't see the image) gets the same information from the
  caption text alone.
- Replaced several symbol-heavy instructions (arrows, slashes) with
  plain words throughout, which is friendlier to screen readers that
  may not announce symbols consistently (see Special Characters,
  below).
- Made every Table of Contents entry a working link to its section,
  supporting readers who navigate with a keyboard or screen reader
  instead of scrolling.

6. DOCUMENT/PAGE FORMATTING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Heading styles are configured so a heading always stays with the
  paragraph that follows it. In the casual guide's layout, a heading
  could land at the bottom of a page with its explanation starting on
  the next page; the compliant version prevents that pairing from ever
  splitting.
- Every image and its caption are grouped as a single unit that can't
  be separated by a page break. This directly fixes a real problem
  found while building the compliant version: an earlier draft let a
  caption ("Bottom gravity." / "Top gravity.") land on the page after
  its image—the final version keeps every figure and caption on the
  same page.
- Moved "5.07 High Score" to begin at the top of a fresh page instead
  of continuing partway down the page after "5.06 Gravity System" ends,
  giving the scoring rules and the Restart subsection their own visual
  space instead of blending into the previous section.
- Promoted "Final Quick Start" out of the bottom of the Troubleshooting
  page and gave it a full page of its own as section "9. Final Quick
  Start," instead of it visually competing for space with the last
  Troubleshooting answer.
- Standardized bullet and numbered list indentation so both marker
  types line up at the same horizontal position, instead of the
  bullets sitting noticeably further left than the numbers.
- Body paragraphs are now fully justified (even left and right
  margins). Note: Microsoft's own accessibility guidance doesn't
  explicitly require justified text, and some accessibility
  authorities (for readers with dyslexia, for example) actually prefer
  left-aligned, ragged-right text because justification can create
  uneven word spacing. This is worth a second look if accessibility for
  low-vision or dyslexic readers is a priority for this document.
- Numbered list items now consistently end with a period after the
  number and after the sentence (for example, "1. Gravity immediately
  reverses."), where the casual guide left both off ("1 Gravity
  immediately reverses").

- Made the Table of Contents a functional navigation aid (hyperlinked
